# Credits

Logoforge stands on the work of these projects and people. Every file's exact source
(project, file, licence) is recorded in [`data/logos.json`](data/logos.json) and shown on
its logo page.

| Source | Licence | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) — pinned in `sources.lock.json` | CC0 1.0 | icons, and most one-color (`icon-mono`) files. Icons that Simple Icons publishes under another licence are excluded. |
| [gilbarbara/logos](https://github.com/gilbarbara/logos) — pinned commit in `sources.lock.json` | CC0 1.0 | most color icons and wordmarks |
| [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/) | public domain (per file) | a few brands no free set covers (see `data/additions/*/source.json`) |
| Brands' own websites | trademark — no licence granted | a few brands with no free vector anywhere (see `data/additions/*/source.json`) |

Tools: [SVGO](https://github.com/svg/svgo) · [SVGR](https://github.com/gregberge/svgr).
